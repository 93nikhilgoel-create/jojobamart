Why Choose Oil Over Seeds

Stop Buying Seeds.
Start Using Oil.

If you're sourcing jojoba seeds for extraction, you're adding cost, risk, and complexity. Here's why buying ready-to-use jojoba oil from us is the smarter decision.

⚙️

No Processing Infrastructure Needed

Seed extraction requires cold-press machinery, filtering equipment, and a skilled workforce. Skip all of this — our oil is ready to formulate or resell, day one.

📊

Consistent Quality, Every Batch

Raw seeds yield variable oil quality depending on harvest, moisture, and storage. Our oil is tested batch-by-batch for purity, colour, and iodine value.

💰

Lower Total Cost of Ownership

Factor in extraction losses (typically 25–35%), equipment depreciation, and labour — buying finished oil costs less and eliminates operational risk entirely.

📜

Compliance & Certifications Ready

Our oil ships with CoA, MSDS, and full regulatory documentation. Getting seeds certified and compliant for your market adds months and significant cost.

🚢

Easier to Import & Transport

Jojoba seeds face more customs scrutiny as agricultural goods. Jojoba oil ships smoothly under harmonised HS codes with simpler phytosanitary requirements.

⏱️

Faster Time-to-Market

From our stock to your production floor in days, not months. Finished oil integrates directly into formulations, eliminating your entire upstream supply chain.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions from B2B buyers sourcing jojoba oil from India.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)? ▾
Our MOQ starts at just 25 litres for most grades — ideal for sampling and initial formulation. For organic certified grades, MOQ is 50 litres. There is no upper limit; we handle bulk FCL shipments regularly.
Do you provide free samples before bulk orders? ▾
Yes — we offer free 100ml samples to qualified B2B buyers (manufacturers, formulators, traders with verifiable business details). You only pay for courier charges. Samples come with the full CoA and MSDS.
What certifications does your jojoba oil carry? ▾
Our oil is ISO 9001:2015 certified, NABL lab tested, and available with USDA Organic, EU Organic, Halal, Kosher, and REACH compliance documentation. We can provide the specific certification package required for your market.
How is the oil packaged and shipped? ▾
We offer 25L, 50L, 200L HDPE/stainless drums, IBC totes (1000L), and flexi tanks for full container loads. We handle all shipping documentation including Bill of Lading, CoA, phytosanitary (if required), and insurance. FOB, CIF, and DAP Incoterms available.
What is the shelf life of your jojoba oil? ▾
Jojoba oil has exceptional stability with a minimum shelf life of 2 years when stored at room temperature away from direct light. Unlike most vegetable oils, jojoba is technically a liquid wax and does not oxidize easily. Our packaging includes a nitrogen flush for extended shelf life.
What payment terms do you accept? ▾
We accept T/T (wire transfer), Letter of Credit (LC), escrow via a trusted third party, and for existing clients, Net 30 payment terms. We understand B2B payment needs and are flexible for large, recurring orders.
Can you supply jojoba seeds instead of oil? ▾
We specialise exclusively in jojoba oil. Based on our experience with hundreds of buyers who initially requested seeds, buying finished oil is more cost-effective, simpler to import, and eliminates the need for extraction machinery. We're happy to walk you through the full cost comparison for your use case.
How quickly can you dispatch after order confirmation? ▾
For in-stock grades, we dispatch within 5–7 business days of payment confirmation. Custom packaging or organic certified grades may require 10–14 days. Airfreight options are available for urgent requirements.
